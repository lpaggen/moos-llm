## Interpretation

- **Base narrative is strongest overall.** It completed every scenario, had 19
  clean passes, only three CPA violations (cases 4, 12, and 13), and the highest
  safety score. Its weakness is citation agreement: it routinely cited Rules
  13/16/19/20 while omitting persistent Rules 2/5/6/7.
- **Eriksen/all-rules nearly ties narrative.** Supplying the reference rules
  produced a 0.952939 citation F1 (2,922 exact cycles out of 3,205). It scored
  lower on safety and physical compliance, however, with 12 CPA-violation
  passes and a case-13 non-arrival capped at 0.20. Citation quality therefore
  did not translate into better physical navigation.
- **Directional narrative SFT v7 is competitive but does not beat the base
  narrative model.** It completed every case and improved citation agreement
  to 0.824767, but 12 of 22 cases violated the 500 m threshold. Its resulting
  0.850140 composite remains below the base narrative model's 0.859067.
- **The final Captain critic does not beat the narrative OOW alone.** It has
  good physical compliance and 13 clean passes, but collisions in cases 1 and
  7 force those cases to zero. Its final Captain decisions also almost never
  cite the persistent reference rules, yielding citation F1 0.000947.
- **The independent Captain experiment is a diagnostic, not the target
  architecture.** Case 19 collided, nine other cases violated CPA, and its
  citation F1 is also effectively zero.
- **Raw JSON state is materially weaker than narrative.** It has three
  non-arrivals (cases 4, 18, and 19), eight additional CPA violations, lower
  safety, and 168 no-action-when-required deductions. Its citation behavior is
  also almost completely mismatched.
- **COLREG summary RAG understands citations better than the unconstrained
  base prompts, but navigates inefficiently.** Its citation F1 is 0.460409, yet
  six non-arrivals are capped at 0.20. Recorded deductions include 33
  wrong-direction turns and 15 port turns toward a contact.
